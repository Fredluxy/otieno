About Walot Enterprise

Walot Enterprise has been a trusted leader in the electrical industry for over 10 years. Founded in 2016, we specialize in providing comprehensive electrical solutions for residential, commercial, and industrial clients across Kenya and East Africa.


Our Story

Starting as a small family business, Walot Enterprise has grown into one of the most reliable electrical service providers in the region. Our journey began with a commitment to quality workmanship and customer satisfaction, values that continue to drive our operations today.


Expertise & Certifications

Our team consists of certified electricians with extensive experience in:

Electrical installations and maintenance

Energy-efficient solutions

Solar power systems

Industrial electrical systems

Emergency electrical repairs

We are licensed and insured, and our technicians hold current certifications from recognized electrical authorities.

Our mission is to deliver safe, efficient, and cost-effective electrical services while maintaining the highest standards of quality and customer satisfaction.
